# FinAI: Building an AI & Analytics Approach to Student Finance in London

![Anil Pandey (Anil Panday) — Creator of FinAI and Business Management with International student at Ravensbourne University London](../anil-pandey-anil-panday-london.jpg)

*By **Anil Panday (Anil Pandey)** — Business Management with International student at Ravensbourne University London, based in London, United Kingdom.*

## The Problem: Why Student Finance Feels Overwhelming

Anyone who has studied in **London, United Kingdom** knows that managing money as a university student is rarely simple. Income often arrives in uneven intervals—maintenance loans, family support, or part-time shifts—while expenses happen every day across rent, transport, groceries, subscriptions, and study materials.

Most students do not lack the desire to budget; they lack **clarity at the moment a financial decision is made**. Traditional spreadsheets require manual data entry that quickly falls behind during busy academic weeks, while basic banking statements show past transactions without helping a student plan the rest of the term.

As a student studying **Business Management with International** at **Ravensbourne University London**, I asked a practical question: *What if we applied the same business analytics and AI automation tools that organisations use for cash-flow forecasting to everyday student finance?*

That question led to **FinAI**.

---

## What Is FinAI?

**FinAI** is my student finance project focused on combining **Python data analysis**, **business analytics principles**, and **artificial intelligence** to help students understand and manage their personal finances more effectively.

Rather than treating budgeting as a static monthly chore, **FinAI** is designed around three practical pillars:

### 1. Clear Expense Categorisation with Python
Students need to see where their money actually goes without spending hours manually labelling rows in a spreadsheet. By structuring transaction data cleanly using **Python**, FinAI organises spending into meaningful student categories: accommodation, transport, food, academic essentials, and discretionary spending.

### 2. Business Analytics Applied to Personal Cash Flow
In **business management**, managers rely on **burn rate**, **runway**, and **variance analysis**. A university student's term budget works on the exact same principles:
- **Runway:** At my current daily spending pace, how many weeks will my balance last?
- **Variance:** Which spending categories are trending above my planned weekly average?
- **Recurring Leaks:** Which small subscriptions or frequent habits quietly add up over a 12-week semester?

FinAI translates these core **business analytics** concepts into straightforward metrics any student can understand at a glance.

### 3. AI-Assisted Financial Guidance and Automation
Where **Artificial Intelligence** and **AI agents** add real value is interpretation. Instead of just displaying a table or chart, an AI workflow can summarise weekly trends in plain English—highlighting when transport or takeaway spending spikes and suggesting realistic adjustments to stay on track for the semester.

## Lessons from Building FinAI

Developing **FinAI** alongside my degree at **Ravensbourne University London** has reinforced three key lessons across technology and business management:

1. **Focus on the real user problem first:** Students do not need complicated financial jargon; they need timely, clear feedback on their weekly budget.
2. **Clean data comes before AI:** Working with **Python and data analysis** demonstrates that any AI assistant is only as reliable as the underlying data pipeline.
3. **Business thinking improves technical projects:** Studying **Business Management with International** helps me approach technical tools with a focus on usability, decision-making, and real-world value.
