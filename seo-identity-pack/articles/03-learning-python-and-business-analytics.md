# Learning Python and Business Analytics as a Business Management Student in London

![Anil Pandey (Anil Panday) — Business Management with International student at Ravensbourne University London](../assets/anil-pandey-anil-panday-london.jpg)

*By **Anil Panday (Anil Pandey)** — Business Management with International student at Ravensbourne University London, based in London, United Kingdom.*

---

## Why Business Students Should Look Beyond Spreadsheets

When I began studying **Business Management with International** at **Ravensbourne University London**, one reality stood out early: almost every modern business decision is also a data decision.

Spreadsheets remain foundational in business management, and every manager needs to understand them well. However, as soon as you want to clean larger datasets, automate recurring reports, test financial scenarios, or connect structured data to **artificial intelligence (AI) agents**, manual spreadsheets begin to show their limits.

That led me to start learning **Python and business analytics** alongside my business management degree in **London, United Kingdom**.

---

## How Business Management Shapes the Way I Learn Python

Learning to code from a **business management** background feels very different from learning programming in isolation. I did not start learning **Python** simply to memorise syntax; I started because I had specific commercial and financial questions I wanted to answer—particularly while developing **FinAI**, my student finance project.

Approaching Python through a business lens changed my workflow in three ways:

### 1. Starting with the Decision, Not the Code
In **business analytics**, a script is only valuable if it helps someone make a clearer decision. Before writing Python code, I define the practical question first:
- *What financial or operational trend are we measuring?*
- *Which variable actually drives the outcome?*
- *How can the output be summarised clearly for decision-making?*

### 2. Automating Repetitive Analytical Tasks
One of the fastest benefits of learning **Python** is workflow automation. Whether standardising CSV transaction files, categorising expenses in **FinAI**, or generating summary metrics, a concise Python script turns a repetitive manual chore into a reliable, repeatable process.

### 3. Connecting Data Analysis with AI Agents
Modern businesses are rapidly adopting **AI agents and automation**. Having a solid foundation in **Python and data analysis** makes it much easier to prepare clean inputs for AI workflows, verify outputs, and combine structured quantitative analysis with natural-language summaries.

---

## My Practical Learning Roadmap

For fellow students in **Business Management with International** or entrepreneurship who want to build fluency in **Python, business analytics, and AI**, these four areas have had the highest impact for me:

1. **Python Fundamentals for Data:** Lists, dictionaries, functions, and working with structured `CSV` and `JSON` files.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Cleaning incomplete records, validating data types, and computing accurate summary statistics.
3. **Business & Financial Metrics:** Translating management concepts—cash flow, runway, unit economics, and budget variance—into reusable Python scripts.
4. **AI Agents & Workflow Automation:** Pairing structured Python data pipelines with AI models to produce clear, actionable insights.
