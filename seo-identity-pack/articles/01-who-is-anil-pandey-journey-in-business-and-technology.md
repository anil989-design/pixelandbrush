## Building FinAI: A Student Finance & Analytics Project

Living and studying in **London** makes one challenge immediately obvious: managing student finance requires constant attention. University students balance irregular income cycles with daily expenses across rent, travel, food, and study materials.

That experience led me to start **FinAI**, a student finance project built around **business analytics, Python data analysis, and artificial intelligence**.

Instead of relying on complicated manual spreadsheets, **FinAI** focuses on:
- Structuring and categorising student expenses clearly.
- Applying core business analytics concepts—such as cash-flow runway and budget variance—to personal student budgets.
- Using AI summarization to give students clear, practical feedback on their weekly spending habits.

Working on **FinAI** allows me to combine what I learn in **Business Management with International** at **Ravensbourne University London** directly with **Python and AI automation**.
