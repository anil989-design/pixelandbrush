## Overview

**FinAI** is an educational and practical student finance initiative developed by **Anil Pandey (Anil Panday)** in **London, United Kingdom**. It combines **Business Analytics**, **Python Data Analysis**, and **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** to help university students track expenses, understand cash-flow runway, and make informed budgeting decisions across the academic term.

---

## Why FinAI Exists

University students in London face irregular income schedules (student maintenance loans, part-time work, bursaries) paired with high, frequent living expenses (accommodation, TfL transport, groceries, academic materials, and subscriptions).

Traditional spreadsheets require manual upkeep, while standard banking views only look backward. **FinAI** applies core **business management and analytics metrics** to personal student finance:

1. **Automated Expense Categorisation (Python):** Structuring raw transaction exports into clear student-specific categories.
2. **Student Cash-Flow Metrics (Business Analytics):**
   - **Term Runway:** Projecting how many weeks a student's current balance will last at their 14-day rolling daily spend rate.
   - **Budget Variance Analysis:** Comparing planned weekly category budgets against actual spending.
   - **Subscription & Recurring Cost Detection:** Identifying small recurring charges that compound over a 12-week semester.
3. **Plain-Language Weekly Insights (AI Agents & Automation):** Turning structured spending tables into concise, actionable weekly summaries without financial jargon.
